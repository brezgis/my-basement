Code borrowed (credited in the files' headers):

- `site/src/furnish/pool_chapel_glass.js` is Vesper Street's chapel glass (`~/projects/town_walk/site/src/chapel/glass.js`), unchanged but for an export added at its end (its generators and tools): Anna's emergence set, drawn as leaded glass on canvas.
- `pool_chapel_fx.js`: the candle flame's fragment shader is `site/src/flame.js`'s; the way the glass is laid out on one sheet and drawn a little at a time, and the ripples in the bowl, follow Vesper Street's `chapel.js` and the house's `garden/ripples.js`.
- `pool_chapel_parts.js`: leaves and palms grown as Frond grows them (`~/projects/town_walk/site/src/shops/plants.js`).
- The lectern's page (the last paragraph of *On the Origin of Species*, first edition, 1859) is set as Vesper Street sets it.

No models, no textures. About 0.16 MB of new pictures (one JPEG).

The pool panel uses [NMW – Römisches Mosaik 1](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NMW_-_R%C3%B6misches_Mosaik_1.jpg), photographed by Wolfgang Sauber, resized for display. The photograph and this resized copy are licensed under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
