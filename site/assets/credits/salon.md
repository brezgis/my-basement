# Assets added by `salon` (The Nail Salon)

None. Everything in the room is built from the kit's geometry in `site/src/furnish/salon_parts.js` and
`salon_fx.js`; the lettering is drawn on canvases. (Two Mucha panels were fetched from Wikimedia Commons and then
removed again when the house's style changed; nothing of them is left in `site/assets/art/`, and the shared
`manifest.json` was never touched.)

Precedent named in salon.js, for the record: the Max Factor Salon, 1666 N. Highland Avenue, Hollywood, remodelled
by S. Charles Lee and reopened in 1935 ("the Jewel Box of the Cosmetic World"; its make-up studio "the Pink Powder
Puff"), per https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Factor_Salon (NRHP contributing property; LAHCM No. 593).
