# The Laundry and The Linen Closet (agent `laundry`)

Assets added (2 files, about 0.45 MB):

- `site/assets/tex/laundry_willow_bough.jpg` (1024 x 1037, 259 kB): **Willow Bough** wallpaper, designed by William
  Morris (1834-1896), 1887; block-printed by Jeffrey & Co. for Morris & Co. From a page of a Morris & Co. sample book,
  1887, Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, object 102200093
  (https://collection.cooperhewitt.org/objects/102200093), via Wikimedia Commons
  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Page_From_Sample_Book,_Morris_and_Co.,_1887_(CH_102200093).jpg).
  **Public domain.** The sample page is a fragment smaller than the print's full repeat, so the tile was made here
  from it: a 17.3 x 17.5 inch piece of the page (the size of the modern reissue's 18 inch repeat), its left and top
  edges re-cut along a least-difference seam against the paper just beyond the right and bottom edges (image
  quilting) so that it tiles. Hung at that real size. It is a faithful piece of the paper, not Morris's exact repeat.
- `site/assets/art/la_hokusai_stretching_cloth.jpg` (1200 x 799): **Women on a Veranda Stretching Cloth to Dry**,
  Katsushika Hokusai, about 1799, colour woodblock print (surimono). Art Institute of Chicago 1925.3292, Clarence
  Buckingham Collection, https://www.artic.edu/artworks/24830. **CC0** (AIC open access). (Made with tools/art.py,
  which also added its line to `site/assets/art/manifest.json`.)

Everything else in the two rooms is drawn in code: the hex mosaic floor, the painted view in the window, the laylight's
frosted glass, the clock's face, the plaque over the door, the sock sign and the closet's basket tags.
