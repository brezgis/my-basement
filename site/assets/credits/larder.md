## Pictures (site/assets/art/)

- `p_kohler_coffee`: *Coffea arabica*, from Köhler's Medizinal-Pflanzen (Gera, 1887-98), chromolithograph; scan by Kurt Stüber via Wikimedia Commons, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Coffea_arabica_-_K%C3%B6hler%E2%80%93s_Medizinal-Pflanzen-189.jpg. Copied from the Laboratory. Public domain. (No longer hung in my rooms after the restyle; `stair_hall.js` uses it, so it stays.)

## The record wall (framed sleeves, The Listening Room)

Twelve record covers, each fetched from its Wikimedia Commons file page (a 500 or 960 px rendering), cropped square and resized to 512 px. Each is marked public domain in the United States on its Commons page, for the reason given; none is a fair-use upload. These are US-law determinations (covers published in the US before 1964 whose copyright was not renewed, or published without a notice).

- `lp_giant_steps`: John Coltrane, *Giant Steps* (Atlantic 1311), 1960; design Marvin Israel, photograph Lee Friedlander. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Coltrane_Giant_Steps.jpg. PD-US-not-renewed.
- `lp_blue_train`: John Coltrane, *Blue Train* (Blue Note 1577), 1958; design Reid Miles, photograph Francis Wolff. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Coltrane_-_Blue_Train.jpg. PD-US-not-renewed (the file page notes that renewal RE0000232299 is for the composition "Blue Train", not the cover).
- `lp_soultrane`: John Coltrane, *Soultrane* (Prestige 7142), 1958; design Esmond Edwards. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Coltrane_Soultrane.jpg. PD-US-not-renewed.
- `lp_takin_off`: Herbie Hancock, *Takin' Off* (Blue Note 84109), 1962. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Takin%27_Off_(1962),_by_Herbie_Hancock.jpg. PD-US-not-renewed.
- `lp_hip_harp`: Dorothy Ashby with Frank Wess, *Hip Harp* (Prestige 7140), 1958. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hip_Harp.jpg. PD-US-not-renewed.
- `lp_black_brown_beige`: Duke Ellington, *Black, Brown and Beige* (RCA Victor album), 1946. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ellington-Black-Brown-Beige-78.png. PD-US-not-renewed.
- `lp_bossa_nova_pops`: Joe Harnell, *Fly Me to the Moon and the Bossa Nova Pops* (Kapp KS-3318), 1963. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Joe_Harnell_Fly_Me_To_The_Moon_Kapp_KS3318_Front_Cover_1_April_2014_5942_800.JPG. PD-US-no-notice (the uploader's photographs of both sides of the sleeve).
- `lp_gershwin_songs`: *George Gershwin Songs, Vol. 1* (Decca album 96), 1939. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:George_Gershwin_Songs,_Vol._1_1939.jpg. PD-US-not-renewed.
- `lp_cole_porter_songs`: *Bing Crosby Sings Cole Porter Songs* (Decca), 1949. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bing_Crosby_Sings_Cole_Porter_Songs_1949.jpg. PD-US-not-renewed.
