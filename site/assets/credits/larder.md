# Assets added by `larder` (The Listening Room, The Cave)

## Models (site/assets/models/)

- `croissant`: single butter croissant (photoscan), copied from Anna's House (`~/projects/my_house/site/assets/models/croissant`). Used only as the croissant carried in the hand (f.help); the ones on show are built in larder_parts.js. Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/croissant. CC0.

## Pictures (site/assets/art/)

- `p_kohler_coffee`: *Coffea arabica*, from Köhler's Medizinal-Pflanzen (Gera, 1887-98), chromolithograph; scan by Kurt Stüber via Wikimedia Commons, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Coffea_arabica_-_K%C3%B6hler%E2%80%93s_Medizinal-Pflanzen-189.jpg. Copied from the Laboratory. Public domain. (No longer hung in my rooms after the restyle; `stair_hall.js` uses it, so it stays.)

The other pictures I had added (van Dijck, Vermeer, van Gogh, Cézanne, Hiroshige) were removed in the restyle: my rooms now hang no pictures. The record sleeves are invented and painted in code.
